01 · Engineering Profile

Hardware fails. Firmware must recover.

I am an Embedded Firmware Engineer based in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. My engineering background bridges 4.5+ years of dedicated low-level Embedded C firmware development at NK Automation with 2 years of hands-on PCB fault analysis and hardware validation at Akarsh Technologies.

Having spent two years desoldering components, tracing PCB schematics, and probing signal lines with digital storage oscilloscopes (DSO) before writing firmware, I bring a unique hardware-first perspective to code design. I write firmware that anticipates power glitches, 24V industrial noise, and bus timing constraints.

At NK Automation, I architect production firmware for PIC18, STM32, and ESP32 microcontrollers powering industrial textile monitors, digital temperature controllers, and Automatic Mains Failure (AMF) generator units. I specialize in register-level driver design (UART, SPI, I2C), non-volatile data logging (EEPROM/RTC), and deterministic Finite State Machines (FSM).
